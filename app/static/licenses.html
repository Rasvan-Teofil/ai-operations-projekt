Current research demo

Qwen3 is the current default and uses Apache-2.0 weights with a fixed zero-shot prompt. MiniLM and reconstructed LinearSVC remain available for research comparison; their training data includes non-commercial restrictions. Publishing this code does not clear those data rights.

The new Wikinews pilot uses AI-labelled, Codex-reviewed references and separate experimental artifacts. Historical models and results remain available. GerNTSA and SENTiVENT below are still proposals. A dataset licence, publisher text rights, a model licence and permission to publish metrics are separate questions.
