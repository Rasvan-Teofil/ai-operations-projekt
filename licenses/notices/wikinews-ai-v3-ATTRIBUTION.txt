Wikinews AI pilot v3 — attribution and scope

Source: German and English Wikinews contributors.
360 revision-pinned headline/lead inputs collected; 342 annotated items retained.
Per-page source, revision, history/author credit, transformations and licences:
https://github.com/Rasvan-Teofil/ai-operations-projekt/blob/second-path/reports/common_data_v3/source_manifest.json

German text: CC BY 2.5. English article-era licence: CC BY 2.5 for older
material and CC BY 4.0 for material published after 16 December 2024.
For later contributions to older English articles, retain both policy terms.
We do not claim that a single licence overrides earlier contribution rights.

Original texts retain their terms; images and quoted leads are excluded.
Only the title and first substantive paragraph are used, with a complete-
sentence lead cap of650characters and metadata removal. Original news text
is kept outside Git; per-page credit and hashes are versioned.

Reference annotations: AI-labelled / Codex-reviewed. The same Codex assistant
created and reviewed these labels before candidate predictions. No human
authorship, annotator consensus or human-gold verification is claimed.
New annotation metadata is offered under CC0-1.0 to the extent project rights
apply; this does not dedicate or relicense underlying Wikinews articles.
Labels and rationales:
https://github.com/Rasvan-Teofil/ai-operations-projekt/blob/second-path/reports/common_data_v3/annotations.csv

Model base MiniLM: Apache-2.0. New models were trained from the original
pretrained base on this new dataset, without legacy restricted fine-tunes.
Artifacts remain local, separate from the historical models.
